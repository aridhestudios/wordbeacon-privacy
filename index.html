Word Beacon - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Word Beacon ("the game") is a word puzzle game published by Aridhe Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location ourselves.

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings and quest progress) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In regions where it is required, the game shows a consent form so you can choose whether ads are personalised. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Sharing and security

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party involved is the ad provider described above. Ad data is sent to it over encrypted connections.

Your choices

You can uninstall the game at any time, which removes the locally saved progress. You can manage ad personalisation as described above.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
